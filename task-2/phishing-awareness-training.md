# Task 2 — Interpret Phishing Simulation Results

## Campaign results

| Team | Email open rate | Click-through rate | Phishing success rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| IT | 80% | 2% | 0% |
| HR | 100% | 85% | 75% |
| Card Services | 60% | 50% | 10% |
| Reception | 40% | 10% | 0% |
| Engineering | 70% | 4% | 1% |
| Marketing | 65% | 40% | 38% |
| R&D | 50% | 5% | 2% |
| Overall average | 66% | 28% | 18% |

## Interpretation

The two teams with the highest phishing success rates are HR at 75% and Marketing at 38%. Both are above the overall phishing success rate of 18%.

HR also recorded the highest click-through rate at 85%, while Marketing recorded 40%.

## Recommended awareness training

### HR
Focus on recognizing urgent account-security requests, identifying impersonation and social-engineering tactics, checking senders and links, reporting suspicious emails, and handling sensitive information safely.

### Marketing
Focus on identifying suspicious links and unexpected requests, recognizing urgency and social engineering, verifying unusual requests through trusted channels, reporting suspected phishing, and applying lessons from recurring simulations.

## Training format
Keep training short, practical, and engaging:
- Brief phishing examples
- Spot-the-red-flags exercises
- Safe link-checking demonstrations
- Clear reporting instructions
- Follow-up simulations to measure improvement

## Success measures
After training, repeat simulations and compare click-through rate, phishing success rate, reporting rate, and results by team.

The goal is to reduce risky interactions and increase appropriate reporting over time.
